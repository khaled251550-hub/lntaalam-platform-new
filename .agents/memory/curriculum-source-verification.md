---
name: Curriculum source verification
description: How to handle Saudi textbook sources when building the curriculum library.
---

Public index pages can link to Google Drive folders for all grades, but the folders are third-party, may show an older file modification date, and do not establish official approval for the current academic year.

**Why:** The official Ain portal requires interactive access, while public mirrors can mix academic years and omit or rename subjects by grade and track.

**How to apply:** Keep source links visibly marked as provisional and unverified; do not claim full-book coverage or redistribute textbook pages until the current PDFs and usage rights are confirmed.
